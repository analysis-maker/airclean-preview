Exceptions

A property counts as ready when all of these are true: the departure clean has been submitted, it is marked complete, the photo review is not a fail unless someone has agreed it, no pre-arrival inspection is still open, no pre-arrival maintenance is still open, and no open urgent maintenance job is on the property. A person can mark the property ready or place a hold, and that choice is logged. If the clean data is more than 3 hours old, or there is no clean record, the result is unknown and early check-in stays closed.
